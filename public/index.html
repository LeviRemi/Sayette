Sayette

Paste or type your script below, then launch the teleprompter. It will listen to you speak and automatically track your position.

Supports markdown formatting. Each paragraph/line becomes a teleprompter segment.

How it works

  • Enter your script above and click "Launch Teleprompter"
  • Allow microphone access when prompted
  • Start speaking naturally — the teleprompter tracks your position
  • You don't need to read verbatim; it uses fuzzy matching to follow along
  • Works best in Chrome or Edge (Web Speech API)
